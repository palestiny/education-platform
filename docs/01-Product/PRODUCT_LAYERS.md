# Product Layers

Date: 2026-09-28
Status: Product Planning Model — PROPOSED
Gate: Product Foundation — NOT PROVEN
Implementation authorization: None

## Purpose

Keep conventional capability parity, advanced learning/operations, AI, and scale economics from becoming one undifferentiated MVP.

## Layer 1 — Foundation

**Context → Learn/Practice → Assess → Evidence → Progress → Next Action → Feedback/Follow-up**

Foundation includes identity, roles/relationships, learner context, content/activity, assignments/practice, assessment/feedback, evidence/progress, teacher workflow, communication, privacy, auditability, localization, accessibility and reliability.

Foundation is a quality baseline, not an MVP commitment.

## Layer 2 — Advanced

Mastery/skills, adaptive assessment, curriculum alignment, actionable analytics, parent visibility, scheduling, attendance, payments and organization operations are selected according to the initial segment and business model.

## Layer 3 — AI

AI sits on top of real product context and evidence:
1. assistive generation;
2. explanation/tutoring;
3. personalized practice;
4. conversational/roleplay;
5. grounded/RAG assistance;
6. controlled actions only where explicitly authorized.

AI requires provenance, evaluation, permission-aware context, safe failure, human accountability and auditability.

## Layer 4 — Scale / Ecosystem

Multi-tenancy, white-label, marketplace, enterprise integrations, advanced BI, content ecosystem, community, broad gamification and credential workflows support expansion and platform economics.

Some influence architecture early without being MVP features.

## Selection Rule

Do not define MVP as “all Foundation features”.

Use:
**Initial Segment + Core Journey + Required Foundation + Segment-Required Advanced + Explicit Quality Bar**

Add AI where it materially improves that journey and can be governed safely. Add scale/ecosystem capabilities according to business and operating-model evidence.

## Research Boundary

Market evidence establishes conventional capability existence/maturity. Direct workflow evidence is reserved for uncertain, segment-specific, materially different, high-impact or differentiating behavior.
