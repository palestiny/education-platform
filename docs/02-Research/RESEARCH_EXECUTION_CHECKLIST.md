# Research Execution Checklist

**Status:** Collection-ready operational checklist  
**Gate:** Product Foundation / Competitive Intelligence — NOT PROVEN  
**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Implementation:** Not authorized

## Purpose

Turn the committed direct-workflow validation protocol into repeatable field execution without inventing evidence or turning documentation into a substitute for participant research.

## Before each session

- [ ] Participant role and segment recorded without unnecessary PII.
- [ ] Voluntary participation explained.
- [ ] Consent/assent and safeguarding requirements checked where applicable.
- [ ] Permission for any artifact review obtained explicitly.
- [ ] No credentials, private keys, account access or unnecessary personal records requested.
- [ ] Case is recent enough to reconstruct concrete events.
- [ ] Interviewer has the case-record template and neutral research script ready.

## During each case reconstruction

- [ ] Record the trigger.
- [ ] Record what evidence actually existed at the time.
- [ ] Record evidence quality and uncertainty.
- [ ] Separate participant interpretation from observed facts.
- [ ] Identify decision owner.
- [ ] Identify action owner.
- [ ] Reconstruct the intervention.
- [ ] Reconstruct communication/context handoff.
- [ ] Record due state and reminders.
- [ ] Reconstruct follow-up.
- [ ] Identify outcome evidence.
- [ ] Identify how closure was known.
- [ ] Capture current workaround and tools/channels used.
- [ ] Capture material cost/friction.
- [ ] Capture failure and recovery if present.
- [ ] Capture privacy/visibility boundaries.
- [ ] Capture AI suggestion/approval boundaries if present.
- [ ] Preserve contradictions instead of resolving them by assumption.

## After each session

- [ ] Assign a case ID.
- [ ] Remove unnecessary identifiers.
- [ ] Classify every important observation as DIRECT OBSERVATION, USER REPORT, ARTIFACT EVIDENCE, MARKET EVIDENCE, INTERPRETATION, or HYPOTHESIS.
- [ ] Record contradictory evidence.
- [ ] Record open questions.
- [ ] Update the cross-case evidence ledger.
- [ ] Do not convert the case into a product requirement.

## After a cohort

Review across cases, without numeric scoring:

1. Recurrence
2. Severity
3. Cost/friction
4. Role/system fragmentation
5. Ownership
6. Follow-up
7. Closure
8. Outcome evidence
9. Evidence quality
10. Privacy/consent
11. Failure/recovery
12. Human accountability
13. Segment fit
14. Existing alternatives
15. Measurable value

## Gate discipline

### PASS
Specific segment + recurring material problem + sufficient evidence for a product decision.

### GAP
Problem is credible but one or more decision-critical dimensions remain unresolved.

### NOT PROVEN
Evidence remains insufficient or too hypothetical.

### REJECT / PIVOT
Evidence materially contradicts the hypothesis or points to a different problem.

A PASS authorizes Product Requirements discovery only. It does not authorize architecture or implementation by itself.

## Stop conditions

Stop or change research direction if:

- cases remain hypothetical;
- the workflow is rare or low-cost;
- current tools already close the loop with little friction;
- ownership/follow-up/closure are already clear;
- the problem is created by the proposed solution rather than existing in practice;
- privacy/safeguarding cannot be handled responsibly;
- evidence consistently points to a different unmet need.

## Evidence integrity rule

**No participant case may be invented, normalized, completed from memory, or filled with assumed details.**

When information is unknown, record it as unknown.

## Current blocker

The repository is collection-ready. The remaining blocking evidence is **real recent cases from actual participants**. Public research and competitor documentation may refine questions, but they cannot close this gate.
