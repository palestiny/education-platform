# Competitive Intelligence Plan

## Objective

Understand the existing education market before locking product scope, architecture or major domain decisions.

Research must identify what existing products actually provide, who they serve, how they monetize, where users repeatedly struggle, what needs remain underserved and which opportunities are supported by evidence.

## Initial Competitor Set

### Egypt / MENA
Nagwa; Noon Academy; Abwaab; almentor; Nafham; Classera; Skolera.

### Global
Khan Academy; Coursera; Udemy; Duolingo; edX; relevant K-12 and learning-platform benchmarks.

This list is a starting point and may change based on evidence.

## Evidence Rules

Every important finding should be tagged VERIFIED FACT, USER REPORT, REPEATED PATTERN, INFERENCE, PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY or OPEN QUESTION.

One user complaint is not enough to establish a systemic weakness.

Repeated patterns should be sought across multiple credible/relevant sources and current product behavior.

## Research Output

1. Competitor matrix
2. Evidence ledger
3. Repeated pain-point map
4. Opportunity map
5. Product differentiation hypotheses
6. Open questions
7. Implications for requirements
8. Implications for domain
9. Implications for UX
10. Implications for architecture

## Gate Rule

Competitive research informs product decisions; it does not silently authorize implementation.

Any major resulting decision must be recorded in the Decision Register and, where architectural, in an ADR.
