## Research correction

The market already contains documented examples of assessment-to-next-step learning workflows and AI-driven personalization. Therefore, “evidence → next action” by itself is not a validated unique opportunity.

The narrower hypothesis now under investigation is:

> Can a cross-role coordination layer connect learner evidence, interpretation, human decision, intervention, follow-up and new evidence across student, parent, teacher and organization while preserving simplicity, trust, privacy and human accountability?

This remains a hypothesis and is not a requirement.

## Evidence interpretation rules

- Official product pages establish documented capabilities, not independent learning outcomes.
- Provider-described workflows establish intended/documented workflow, not proof of user effectiveness.
- A feature absent from a public page is not proof of absence.
- A single complaint remains a USER REPORT.
- Multiple reports may establish an early pattern, not automatically a systemic weakness.
- No competitor is ranked, scored or declared a winner.

## Current conclusion

Feature breadth is crowded. Single-product personalization and assessment-to-next-step workflows are also already documented in the market. The next research pass therefore focuses on cross-role intervention coordination, evidence provenance/confidence, human decision ownership, closure/follow-up, reliability/recovery, privacy/visibility and measurable value.
