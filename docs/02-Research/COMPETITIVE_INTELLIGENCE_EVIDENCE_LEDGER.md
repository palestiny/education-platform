## Important Interpretation Rules

- Official product pages establish what a company currently says its product provides; they do not independently prove learning outcomes.
- Company-reported user/teacher scale is recorded as company-reported, not independently verified.
- A product feature being absent from a public page does not prove the product lacks it.
- A single complaint or review will not be treated as a systemic weakness.
- We will not label a competitor's feature or product a “weakness” without sufficient evidence.

## First Research Conclusion

The market is already broad. Existing products demonstrate capabilities across:

- content and courses,
- question banks and assessment,
- live interaction,
- social learning,
- parent visibility,
- teacher tooling,
- AI assistance,
- institutional/LMS functionality.

The current evidence therefore supports **continuing discovery around coordination, evidence, decision support and end-to-end learning orchestration**, rather than assuming that adding more isolated features is the differentiator.

This is not yet sufficient to close the Competitive Intelligence Gate.
