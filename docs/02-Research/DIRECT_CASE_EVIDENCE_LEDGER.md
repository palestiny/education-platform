## Purpose

Maintain a cross-case evidence record for direct workflow validation. This ledger is for synthesis, not scoring participants or ranking segments.

## Evidence rules

- One row/case is evidence of that case only.
- Do not convert repeated reports into prevalence without a defined sample and denominator.
- Preserve contradictory evidence.
- Separate observed facts, user reports, artifacts, market evidence, and researcher interpretation.
- Remove unnecessary personally identifying information.
- Never store credentials, private messages, or sensitive student records in this repository.
- Use case IDs instead of names.

## Cross-case synthesis

### Frequency

Record how many collected cases contain the workflow and how many do not. Do not claim market prevalence from this sample.

### Material cost or friction

Capture concrete time, duplicate work, delays, missed follow-ups, communication overhead, or other observable consequences.

### Fragmentation

Record where evidence, context, ownership, or action crosses tools, people, or organizational boundaries.

### Ownership

Record whether decision ownership and action ownership were explicit, ambiguous, duplicated, or missing.

### Follow-up and closure

Record whether next steps had an owner and due state, whether follow-up happened, and how closure was established.

### Outcome evidence

Record whether the participant could demonstrate improvement, no change, uncertainty, or no measurement.

### Evidence quality

Record whether decisions relied on direct observation, assessments, attendance, reports, memory, or other evidence, and whether evidence was stale, missing, or contradictory.

### Workaround

Record existing tools and manual processes before considering a product intervention.

### Failure and recovery

Record what happened when a tool, communication channel, person, or process failed.

### Privacy and consent

Record role boundaries, parent visibility, student autonomy, consent requirements, and inappropriate-access risks.

### AI and human accountability

Record whether automation or AI was involved and where human review, override, or accountability remained.

## Decision evidence summary

Do not produce a numeric score or overall ranking. Summarize:

- What is directly supported?
- What remains uncertain?
- What evidence argues against the hypothesis?
- What evidence is segment-specific?
- What existing alternatives already solve the problem?
- What value could be measured if the hypothesis survives?

## Gate handoff

Before Product Foundation review, link the completed case records and document:

1. Cases collected and participant-role coverage.
2. Recurring workflow patterns observed.
3. Material cost/friction evidence.
4. Current alternatives and failure points.
5. Ownership/follow-up/closure evidence.
6. Outcome evidence.
7. Privacy/consent boundaries.
8. Failure/recovery evidence.
9. AI/human accountability evidence.
10. Contradictory evidence.
11. Remaining open questions.

A gate cannot be marked PASS merely because the ledger contains several cases. The evidence must establish a meaningful, segment-specific problem and a measurable value hypothesis while surviving contradictory evidence.
