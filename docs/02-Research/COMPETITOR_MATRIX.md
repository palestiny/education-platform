## Verified market observations

1. Existing products already cover substantial parts of content delivery, practice, assessment, live interaction, parent progress visibility, teacher support, AI assistance, and institutional management.
2. Khan Academy explicitly provides a parent dashboard with child progress, assignments, and activity reports.
3. UNESCO's 2023 GEM report emphasizes that technology should be judged by educational objectives and evidence, not by technology adoption alone.
4. Therefore, adding isolated features is not yet a validated differentiation strategy.

## Important evidence limits

- Public competitor pages establish what a company says it provides; they do not independently establish learning outcomes.
- Company-reported scale figures are recorded as company claims.
- Absence of a feature from a public page is not proof that the feature does not exist.
- Unverified cells remain explicitly marked as requiring research.
- This matrix is descriptive, not a ranking.

## Research implication

The next research question is not which competitor has the most features.

It is:

> Where do students, parents, teachers, and educational organizations still need coordinated decision support across the full learning journey?

Candidate hypothesis for validation:

Current State -> Evidence -> Interpretation -> Next Best Action -> Intervention -> New Evidence

This remains a PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY hypothesis, not a product decision.
