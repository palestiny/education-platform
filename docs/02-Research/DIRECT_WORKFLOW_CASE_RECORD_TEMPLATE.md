# Direct Workflow Case Record Template

**Status:** Research Instrument  
**Gate:** Product Foundation / Competitive Intelligence — NOT PROVEN  
**Implementation:** Not authorized

## Use

Use this record only for a real recent case reconstructed with a participant or observed from an authorized artifact. Do not invent or normalize missing details.

Do not collect unnecessary personally identifiable information. Use a Case ID rather than names.

## 1. Trigger

- What first caused someone to notice the issue?
- Who noticed it?
- When?

## 2. Evidence

- What evidence was available?
- Where did it come from?
- Was it complete, recent, consistent and understandable?
- What evidence was missing?

## 3. Interpretation

- What did the participant believe the evidence meant?
- Was there uncertainty or disagreement?
- What alternative explanations were considered?

## 4. Decision Ownership

- Who was responsible for deciding what should happen?
- Was that responsibility explicit?
- Was a decision delayed or passed between people?

## 5. Action Ownership

- Who actually performed the action?
- Was ownership explicit?
- Did the action owner have the required authority/context?

## 6. Intervention

- What was actually done?
- Was it instructional, behavioral, attendance-related, communication-related, administrative, or another intervention?
- What was the intended outcome?

## 7. Communication

- Who needed to know?
- Which channels were used?
- Was context preserved across channels?
- Did communication create duplicate work or conflicting information?

## 8. Due State

- Was there a deadline, reminder, next-check date, or expected follow-up?
- Who was expected to remember it?
- What happened if the due point was missed?

## 9. Follow-up

- Was follow-up performed?
- By whom?
- How was the follow-up triggered?
- Was the original context still available?

## 10. Outcome Evidence

- What evidence showed improvement, no improvement, failure, or unresolved status?
- When was that evidence collected?
- Could the participant distinguish activity from actual outcome?

## 11. Closure

- How did participants know the case was resolved?
- Was closure explicit?
- If unresolved, how was that state represented?

## 12. Workaround

Record actual tools/processes used:

- Messaging / WhatsApp
- Phone call
- Spreadsheet
- Paper notes
- Calendar/reminder
- Existing education platform
- Memory
- Other:

For each, record what it solved and what it did not solve.

## 13. Cost / Friction

Capture concrete evidence where possible:

- Time spent:
- Number of people involved:
- Number of tools/channels:
- Repeated work:
- Missed action:
- Missed learning opportunity:
- Financial cost:
- Trust/relationship cost:
- Other material consequence:

## 14. Failure / Recovery

- What failed?
- How was failure detected?
- Could the workflow resume?
- Was context lost?
- Was a person required to reconstruct the case manually?
- What recovery mechanism was used?

## 15. Privacy / Consent

- What information was visible to each role?
- What information should not have been visible?
- Was consent required?
- Was consent actually present?
- Did communication create privacy risk?

## 16. AI / Human Boundary

- Was AI involved?
- What did AI produce?
- Who interpreted or verified it?
- Who approved the final educational action?
- What happened when evidence was weak, stale, missing, or contradictory?
