## Important Semantic Boundary

The loop does not treat activity as learning, an interpretation as evidence, a recommendation as a decision, communication as follow-up, or completed follow-up as proof of outcome.

The exact progress calculation, mastery model, intervention domain, parent semantics, tenancy model and AI behavior remain open/conditional.
