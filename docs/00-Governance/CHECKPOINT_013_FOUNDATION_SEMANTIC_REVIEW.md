## Key Result

The three candidate segments share a meaningful semantic core:

**Context → Goal → Learning Action → Evidence → Interpretation → Progress → Next Useful Action → Feedback / Follow-up → New Evidence**

However, the review does not justify treating ownership, parent participation, organization context, evidence authority, scheduling, attendance, payments, intervention lifecycle, progress calculation or AI behavior as identical across segments.

## New Guardrails

1. Operational signals must not automatically become learning evidence.
2. Parent is not a universal semantic role; visibility and action rights remain context-dependent.
3. Follow-up does not automatically require a first-class intervention/case domain.
4. Progress semantics are not defined merely by the existence of the learning loop.
5. External systems may be authoritative for some evidence/context.
6. Recommendation remains distinct from decision.
7. No direct case evidence exists yet for the proposed differentiating intervention workflow.
