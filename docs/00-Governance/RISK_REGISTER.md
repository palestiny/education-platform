# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Status | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RISK-0001 | Scope expands faster than product evidence. | High | Medium | OPEN | Gates, research and explicit MVP boundaries |
| RISK-0002 | Architecture is prematurely optimized for scale. | High | Medium | OPEN | Architecture Gate and evidence-based scaling decisions |
| RISK-0003 | Product becomes complex for users while remaining technically sophisticated. | High | Medium | OPEN | UX Gate and simplicity principle |
| RISK-0004 | AI produces unsupported recommendations. | High | Medium | OPEN | Evidence-first design, evaluation and human accountability |
| RISK-0005 | Parent visibility becomes surveillance rather than useful confidence. | High | Medium | OPEN | Product/UX/privacy design and explicit consent |
| RISK-0006 | Multi-tenant data isolation is insufficient. | Critical | Low/Unknown | OPEN | Security and data architecture gates |
| RISK-0007 | Global requirements are hard-coded around initial market assumptions. | High | Medium | OPEN | Global-by-architecture principle |
| RISK-0008 | Video/live infrastructure creates unnecessary operational burden. | High | Medium | OPEN | External-provider-first investigation |
| RISK-0009 | Requirements are invented during coding. | High | Medium | OPEN | Working Rules and traceability |
| RISK-0010 | Defects are patched without identifying root cause. | High | Medium | OPEN | Root-cause workflow and regression verification |
